  • 원본 A열 (Curr.) → 템플릿 A열 (Currency)
  • 원본 D열 (Customer Name) → 템플릿 B열 (Customer Name)
  • 원본 E열 (Description) → 템플릿 C열 (Description)
  • 원본 F열 (Deposit Amt) → 템플릿 D열 (Deposit Amount)
  • 원본 G열 (Fee) → 템플릿 E열 (Fee)
  • 원본 H열 (Exchange Rate) → 템플릿 F열 (Exchange Rate)
  • 원본 I열 (Deposit KRW) → 템플릿 G열 (Deposit KRW)
